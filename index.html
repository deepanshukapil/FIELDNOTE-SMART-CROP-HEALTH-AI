READ THE SIGNAL, NOT JUST THE SCORE

What these models can—and can’t—tell you

AI output is a clue for further inspection. It is not a diagnosis or a promise of yield.

A

Crop model

Trained on 2,200 labeled records across 22 crops. It reached 99% accuracy on a random holdout from that dataset; this score does not establish local farm accuracy. The displayed risk band is a model-probability signal, not calibrated crop-failure risk.

B

Leaf classifier

Trained on 3,800 curated PlantVillage images across 38 classes. Best validation accuracy was 67.2% on a 760-image split from the same source. Field lighting, crop variety, and real symptoms can differ substantially.

C

Use with care

Low confidence or a poor-quality image should prompt another photo or expert review. For treatment, pesticide, or planting decisions, consult a local agronomist and use region-specific evidence.
